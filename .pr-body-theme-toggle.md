## Summary

Adds a dark/light mode toggle to the toolbar.

- New sun/moon button at the right of the toolbar switches `appTheme` between `light` and `dark` (overriding the system appearance).
- The icon reflects the current effective appearance: moon in light mode, sun in dark mode.
- The setting persists via `@AppStorage` and applies window-wide through the existing `preferredColorScheme` wiring.

Build and 40 tests pass; verified live — clicking the toggle flips the whole UI between light and dark.
